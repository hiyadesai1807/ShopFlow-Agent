VOLTKART STORE POLICIES

VoltKart is a fictional online electronics store created for this demo project. All policies below are made up for practice.

RETURN POLICY

Most products can be returned within 10 days of delivery. The product must be unused, in its original packaging, and returned with all accessories, manuals and the invoice.

Headphones and earbuds can be returned only if the seal is unopened, for hygiene reasons. If the seal is opened, they can be replaced only when they are defective.

Laptops, smartphones, tablets and televisions have a 7 day replacement-only policy. They cannot be returned for a refund unless the replacement is also defective.

Products bought during a clearance sale are final sale and cannot be returned. They are still covered by warranty.

To start a return, go to My Orders, select the order and choose Return or Replace. A pickup is scheduled within 2 working days.

REFUND POLICY

Refunds are started after the returned product passes a quality check at our warehouse. The quality check takes up to 2 working days after pickup.

Refunds to UPI and wallets take 1 to 2 working days. Refunds to credit cards, debit cards and net banking take 5 to 7 working days.

For cash on delivery orders, the refund is sent to the bank account you provide, and takes 5 to 7 working days.

Shipping charges are refunded only when the product is defective, damaged or different from what was ordered.

SHIPPING POLICY

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days in metro cities and 5 to 8 working days in other locations.

Standard delivery is free on orders of Rs. 499 or more. Orders below Rs. 499 have a shipping charge of Rs. 49.

Express delivery takes 1 to 2 working days, is available only in metro cities and costs Rs. 99 per order.

Televisions and other large products are delivered by a separate courier partner and include free installation scheduling within 2 days of delivery.

We currently deliver only within India.

ORDER CANCELLATION

An order can be cancelled free of charge any time before it is shipped. Go to My Orders and choose Cancel.

Once an order is shipped, it cannot be cancelled. You can refuse the delivery or start a return after it arrives.

For cancelled prepaid orders, the full amount is refunded within 5 to 7 working days.

WARRANTY POLICY

All products come with a manufacturer warranty. Smartphones, laptops, tablets and televisions have a 1 year warranty. Headphones, speakers, wearables and accessories have a 6 month warranty.

Warranty covers manufacturing defects only. It does not cover physical damage, liquid damage, or damage from unauthorised repair.

To claim warranty, keep your invoice and contact the brand's service centre. Our support team can help you find the nearest centre.

An extended warranty of 1 extra year can be bought within 30 days of delivery for smartphones, laptops and televisions.

PAYMENT OPTIONS

We accept UPI, credit cards, debit cards, net banking and cash on delivery.

Cash on delivery is available on orders up to Rs. 20,000 and has a handling fee of Rs. 40.

No-cost EMI is available on orders of Rs. 5,000 or more with selected credit cards, for 3 or 6 months.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customer support is available from 9 AM to 9 PM, Monday to Saturday, through chat and email at support@voltkart.example.

Complaints are answered within 24 hours. If a complaint is not resolved in 7 days, it can be escalated to the grievance officer through the Help page.
